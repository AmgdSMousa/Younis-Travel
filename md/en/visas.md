# Visa Services

A visa rejection usually happens because of a small mistake in the application or a booking that is not right. Our specialist team prepares your file properly, for the highest acceptance rates.

Younis Travel issues electronic visas for countries such as Turkey, Vietnam, Albania, Kenya and the UAE, and books embassy and visa-center appointments for the USA, Schengen, the United Kingdom and Saudi Arabia (Tasheer). A specialist team reviews and fully prepares your file — application, employment letters, certified translation and confirmed preliminary bookings — because a visa rejection usually comes down to one small avoidable mistake.
