Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start booking?

Send us a message on the Facebook Page, or fill in the request form here with the destination, dates and number of travelers, and we will reply with the details and available options.

What documents does a visa need?

It depends on the country and visa type, but the basics are usually: a valid passport, recent personal photos, and confirmed preliminary flight and hotel bookings. Send us your destination country and we will send you the exact document list for your file.

How does payment work?

Once you confirm the offer that suits you, payment is available through easy, multiple methods: cash, bank transfer, or electronic payment — and you receive your ticket in minutes on WhatsApp and email.

Can I change or cancel my booking?

We give you full support for changing dates, upgrading seats or cancelling, under the airline or hotel conditions — without the complications of dealing with them directly.

Do the hotel receipts work for embassies?

Yes — we issue booking receipts and official invoices that are fully accepted at embassies and airports.

Is travel insurance really important?

It is required by many embassies, and it is also one of the documents that strengthens your visa file — besides covering you comprehensively throughout your trip.
